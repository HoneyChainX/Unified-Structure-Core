10. SPREAD INTELLIGENCE ENGINE
========================================================

PURPOSE:
Measure:
- liquidity rotation
- altcoin strength
- stablecoin pressure
- BTC relative strength

--------------------------------------------------------
A) ALT ROTATION
--------------------------------------------------------

spread_alt_rot =
log(total3exbtc / btcCap)
-
sma(log(total3exbtc / btcCap), L)

--------------------------------------------------------
B) RELATIVE STRENGTH VS BTC
--------------------------------------------------------

spread_x_btc =
log(close / btcUsd)
-
sma(log(close / btcUsd), L)

--------------------------------------------------------
C) STABLECOIN PRESSURE
--------------------------------------------------------

spread_stable =
log((usdtCap + usdcCap)/total3)
-
sma(log((usdtCap + usdcCap)/total3), L)

--------------------------------------------------------
FINAL SPREAD SCORE
--------------------------------------------------------

SPREAD_SCORE =
0.4 * spread_alt_rot
+
0.3 * spread_x_btc
+
0.3 * spread_stable

--------------------------------------------------------
BOT INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------

Positive spread:
risk-on environment

Negative spread:
defensive environment
