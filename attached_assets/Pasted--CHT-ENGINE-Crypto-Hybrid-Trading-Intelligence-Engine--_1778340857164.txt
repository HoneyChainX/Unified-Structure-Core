========================
CHT ENGINE
(Crypto Hybrid Trading Intelligence Engine)
========================

PURPOSE:
CHT Engine is a multi-factor AI trading decision framework designed for crypto opportunity detection.
The engine evaluates:
- Trend
- Market Structure
- Liquidity Rotation
- Volatility
- Volume
- Risk
- Multi-Timeframe Alignment
- Context
- Spread Intelligence
- Confirmation Consensus

The engine DOES NOT enter trades based on a single signal.
A trade is valid ONLY if all major engines align.

========================================================
2. TREND ENGINE
========================================================

PRIMARY TREND:
EMA_FAST = EMA(20)
EMA_SLOW = EMA(50)

BULLISH TREND:
EMA20 > EMA50

BEARISH TREND:
EMA20 < EMA50

TREND STRENGTH:
ADX(14) > 18

IF ADX < 18:
MARKET_STATE = RANGE

--------------------------------------------------------
MULTI-TIMEFRAME CONFIRMATION
--------------------------------------------------------

CURRENT TF -> HIGHER TF

5m  -> 1h
15m -> 1h
1h  -> 4h
4h  -> 1D

LONG CONDITIONS:
TF Trend = Bullish
AND
HTF Trend = Bullish

SHORT CONDITIONS:
TF Trend = Bearish
AND
HTF Trend = Bearish

========================================================
3. MARKET STRUCTURE ENGINE (MSS / BOS)
========================================================

PURPOSE:
Detect real structure shifts and breakout confirmations.

--------------------------------------------------------
BULLISH MSS
--------------------------------------------------------

Price closes above confirmed Swing High.

--------------------------------------------------------
BEARISH MSS
--------------------------------------------------------

Price closes below confirmed Swing Low.

--------------------------------------------------------
VALID BREAKOUT REQUIREMENTS
--------------------------------------------------------

1. Candle CLOSE beyond level.
2. Wick-only breakout is invalid.
3. Candle body size > 40% of candle range.
4. Volume confirmation required.

--------------------------------------------------------
RESET LOGIC
--------------------------------------------------------

If opposite MSS appears:
- previous MSS becomes invalid
- trend state resets immediately

========================================================
4. TRIGGER ENGINE
========================================================

SUPPORTED SETUPS:
1. BREAKOUT
2. RETEST
3. REVERSAL

--------------------------------------------------------
A) BREAKOUT TRIGGER
--------------------------------------------------------

LONG:
Close > Resistance
AND
Volume > SMA(volume,20) * 1.2
AND
Bullish Trend

SHORT:
Close < Support
AND
Volume Confirmed
AND
Bearish Trend

--------------------------------------------------------
B) RETEST TRIGGER (HIGH PRIORITY)
--------------------------------------------------------

LONG FLOW:
1. Breakout occurs
2. Price revisits breakout level
3. Bullish rejection appears
4. Candle closes above level

SHORT FLOW:
Opposite logic.

RETEST trades have:
- higher quality
- lower fakeouts
- better RR

--------------------------------------------------------
C) REVERSAL TRIGGER
--------------------------------------------------------

LONG:
Price at Demand Zone
AND
RSI crosses above 50
AND
Bullish confirmation candle

SHORT:
Price at Supply Zone
AND
RSI crosses below 50
AND
Bearish confirmation candle

========================================================
8. SPREAD INTELLIGENCE ENGINE
========================================================

PURPOSE:
Measure:
- Altcoin strength
- Liquidity rotation
- Stablecoin pressure
- Relative strength vs BTC

--------------------------------------------------------
SPREAD EQUATIONS
--------------------------------------------------------

A) ALT ROTATION SPREAD

spread_alt_rot =
log(total3exbtc / btcCap)
-
sma(log(total3exbtc / btcCap), L)

--------------------------------------------------------
B) RELATIVE STRENGTH VS BTC

spread_x_btc =
log(close / btcUsd)
-
sma(log(close / btcUsd), L)

--------------------------------------------------------
C) STABLECOIN PRESSURE

spread_stable =
log((usdtCap + usdcCap) / total3)
-
sma(log((usdtCap + usdcCap) / total3), L)

--------------------------------------------------------
FINAL SPREAD SCORE
--------------------------------------------------------

SPREAD_SCORE =
0.4 * spread_alt_rot
+
0.3 * spread_x_btc
+
0.3 * spread_stable

--------------------------------------------------------
INTERPRETATION
--------------------------------------------------------

HIGH POSITIVE SCORE:
- Strong alt rotation
- Bullish liquidity flow

HIGH NEGATIVE SCORE:
- Stablecoin defensive flow
- Risk-off environment

11. DIVERGENCE ENGINE
========================================================

IMPORTANT:

It only increases confidence score.
